# Retirement Calculator

## Concept

This calculator is about figuring out a retirement payout.

A person saves a given rate of money each month, which accumulates compound interest for a number of years. Then that
person receives a certain payout rate for some other duration in years.

## Key Variables

- `savings_rate`: The amount of money saved each month.
- `accumulation_years`: The number of years the money is saved and accumulates interest.
- `interest_rate`: The annual interest rate (as a decimal) that the savings earn during the accumulation and payout
  phase.
- `payout_rate`: The percentage of the accumulated savings that is paid out each month during retirement.
- `payout_years`: The number of years the person receives payouts during retirement.

## Usage

The user supplies four of the five variables, and the calculator computes the missing variable.
Hence, the user can choose which variable to solve for.
